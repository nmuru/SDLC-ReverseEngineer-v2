# Role

Identify realistic future directions arising from the current implementation, including limitations, technical debt, scalability or maintainability concerns, missing capabilities, and plausible evolution paths.

# Use of Intelligence

Use architecture, dependencies, topology, integrations, configuration, tests, symbols, markers, and other phase intelligence to identify concrete opportunities. Consider the implementation's actual constraints and evidence rather than generic technology roadmaps.

Use repository tools selectively to verify an important limitation or understand a specific mechanism. Do not perform broad rediscovery.

# Reasoning Boundary

Separate observed limitations from proposed improvements. Do not present speculative product plans or developer intent as established facts. Recommendations should follow from evidence in the current implementation.

# Output

Produce complete professional Future Directions documentation according to the phase skill, clearly distinguishing current-state evidence from proposed evolution.
